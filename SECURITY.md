2. **Direct Reporting:** Send a secure, encrypted report directly to the authorized administrator and developer profile: **@YassinService**.
3. **Response Timeline:** 
   * Acknowledgement of receipt: Within **24 hours** by the grace of Allah.
   * Assessment and Validation: Within **3 business days**.
   * Resolution Deployment: Critical hotfixes will be committed directly via clean manual branch updates to the main repository.
